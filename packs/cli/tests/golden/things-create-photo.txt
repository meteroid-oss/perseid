Add a photo

`POST /v1/things/{thing_id}/photos`

Usage: acme things create-photo [OPTIONS] --file <FILE> <THING_ID>

Arguments:
  <THING_ID>
          The thing's ID

Options:
      --caption <STRING>
          Shown under the photo

      --file <FILE>
          (required) (a path, or - for stdin)

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Global options: --format --jq --pretty --max-items --all --dry-run --allow-unknown-fields --profile --api-key --base-url --retries --no-retry --timeout --no-color --debug; see `acme --help`
