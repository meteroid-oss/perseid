A usage report

`GET /v1/reports`

Usage: acme reports retrieve --since <DATE> --kind <STRING>

Options:
      --since <DATE>
          (required) First day of the report

      --kind <STRING>
          (required)
          
          [possible values: daily, monthly]

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')

Example:
  acme reports retrieve --since 2026-01-31 --kind daily

Global options: --format --jq --pretty --max-items --all --dry-run --allow-unknown-fields --profile --api-key --base-url --retries --no-retry --timeout --no-color --debug; see `acme --help`
